## What Paperhint is

An AI teaching assistant for schools. The centre of gravity is the
teacher's daily grind — evaluating, preparing, assigning — done by AI
and approved by the teacher. School-management modules exist around it,
but they are the supporting cast, not the pitch.

Mission: put the application on every desk so schools work more
advanced, productively.

## Primary modules

1. **AI answer-sheet evaluation** (the crown jewel)
   - Companion mobile app exists today; its single purpose: photograph /
     scan answer sheets and upload them.
   - The product analyses the whole PDF and marks each answer right or
     wrong against the provided question.
   - Scoring is mechanical and automatic, driven by a **rubric system**.

2. **AI question paper creation**
   - Generated from the syllabus, and from the teacher's own notes
     library. This is a major reason the library exists.

3. **AI homework creation**
   - Teacher generates homework; creation triggers the parent
     notification flow (see 6).

4. **AI teaching / class notes**
   - Teacher asks AI to create class notes or teaching notes they can
     actually teach with. Emphasis on producing *productive* notes.

5. **The Library (personal repository, shareable)**
   - Every note and reference saves into the teacher's own repository
     ("my notebook").
   - Notes can be kept private or **shared publicly** — usable by anyone,
     from any class.
   - The library feeds everything: teaching, question papers, homework.

6. **Parent connect (notification system)**
   - The moment homework is created, parents are notified: "this is the
     homework given to your kid" — so parents drive completion at home.
   - Today this uses a WhatsApp-group-style template; the direction is
     teachers connecting directly with parents.

7. **Attendance (teacher module)**
   - Two paths: mark attendance on the usual paper/graph sheet, then
     scan & upload it (the product reads it in), OR manage it manually
     in-app (drop / increase).

8. **Admin / School portal** (people & structure management)
   - Admins create and manage the school's structure: **classes,
     sections, students, teachers** — the administrative people-management
     layer ("model base management").
   - **Subject management**: admins add subjects and the subject books /
     materials available for each; those become accessible to all the
     respective department's teachers (feeding their libraries and AI
     generation).
   - Primary admin modules: teacher management · classroom management ·
     section management · student management · subject management.

## Supporting modules ("fruitful for the school")

Timetables, academics/marks records, calendar — the school OS around the
teaching core. Present, useful, secondary in the story.
