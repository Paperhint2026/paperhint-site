ABOUT Note from the founders ·

Why we built this

Dear schools that raised us,

We were your students once. We sat in your classrooms, lined up in your corridors, and carried home the report cards you wrote by hand. Long before Paperhint was an idea, you were already doing the work — quietly, year after year.

And because we were there, we saw what it cost. We watched our teachers stay back after the last bell to redraw a timetable someone's absence had broken. We saw marks copied from register to register until midnight, notes photocopied until the ink gave out, and principals holding entire academic years together with little more than patience and a filing cabinet. Years later we came back as visitors, and nothing had changed — the same invisible work, still carried by hand.

Somewhere in those visits the thought took hold: the people who gave us everything were spending their evenings on work no one ever thanked them for. We wanted to give something back — not a tribute, but time. That is when this idea found us.

Paperhint is our way of returning what you spent on us. The answer sheets that mark themselves against your rubric, the question paper drafted in minutes for your syllabus, the parent who hears about homework the moment it's assigned — every one of these is an evening handed back to someone who once handed theirs to us.

If your school still runs on the invisible machine, let us show you the same term running on one page. It would be an honour.

— with gratitude, the Paperhint team
