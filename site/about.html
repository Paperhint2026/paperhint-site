ABOUT A note from the founder ·

Why we built this

Chennai, 2026

Dear teachers, principals, and everyone holding a school together,

Every school we visited ran on the same invisible machine: a timetable rebuilt by hand each term, marks copied between registers and spreadsheets, notes photocopied until the ink gave up, and a WhatsApp group where homework went to disappear. None of it was anyone's actual job. All of it ate the hours that were supposed to go to teaching.

Paperhint started as a simple question — what if the paperwork could hint at doing itself? Not another portal that adds work, but one place where the timetable resolves its own clashes, a question paper drafts itself from a teacher's own notes, and a parent hears about marks before they have to ask.

We are building it with schools, not just for them. Every screen in this product exists because a teacher showed us the register it replaces.

If your school still runs on the invisible machine, we would love to show you the same term running on one page.

— The Paperhint team
