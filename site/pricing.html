Questions we get asked

Are teachers and parents charged separately?

No. Pricing is per enrolled student, and that covers every account the school needs — admins, teachers, students and parent logins included.

Can we move our existing data across?

Yes. Student records, staff details, past academics and existing notes can be migrated. On the School and District plans migration is done for you as part of onboarding.

What happens between academic years?

Paperhint rolls a year over rather than resetting it. Sections are promoted, the library and question banks carry forward, and the previous year stays available as a record.

Do parents need to install anything?

No. Parent updates on marks, homework and performance arrive by email, triggered by the teacher who shares them.

Is there a mid-year start?

Yes — schools commonly start mid-term with timetables and parent notifications first, then move academics across at the term break. Billing is pro-rated to your academic year.
